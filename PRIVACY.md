# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Daily Intake is open-source software that you run yourself, on your own server. This policy covers
the software in this repository and the Google access it asks for.

## Who sees your data

Only you. Daily Intake has no central service: every copy runs on the server of the person who
installed it, and the project's authors never receive anything from it. There are no accounts, no
analytics, no ads and no tracking.

## Google data it reads

- **Google Calendar**: the dashboard reads your recent and upcoming events, read-only
  (`calendar.readonly`), to show today's timeline. The AI agent you connect (for example Hermes) can
  also book work blocks on your calendar, only when you ask it to.
- **Gmail**, read-only, through the AI agent you connect (for example Hermes): new mail, to build the
  Gmail summary. It keeps only the sender's name and subject of threads waiting for a reply, the
  store and status of packages on the way, the merchant and time of payments (never amounts), and how
  many messages were deleted or archived. It never sends, deletes or changes mail.

## Where it is kept

In your own database, on your own server. Daily Intake doesn't copy it anywhere else.

## Sharing

Your data is never sold, never shared with anyone, and never used for advertising or to train AI
models. The AI agent you connect reads it with the model you set it up to use, under that agent's
own settings.

Daily Intake's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing access

You can revoke Daily Intake's Google access at any time at
[myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Deleting your database
deletes everything it stored.
